### Correction 1 — Senna's age and gender are missing

> **Line:** subtitle — `Archive reconstruction · Free Latitude dock records and
> third-party combat reports · revised 43 CE`
> **And:** ¶1 through ¶6 contain no statement of her gender or current age.

Every other pilot entry in the Archive carries gender and age, and the sample's
own `pyre` entry does it correctly in ¶1: *"Contract pilot, woman, mid 20s."*
`nulltide` drops both. Gender survives only as pronouns; age appears only as
historical figures at certification and trial.

Brief §3.3 fixes these as canon and the portrait spec is authoritative: **Io
Senna, woman, 30s.**

> **Required correction.** State both. The reconstruction framing makes this
> *better*, not worse — the entry already says "Cross-referenced ages and dates
> place Senna at twenty-four on the eleventh of the fourth, 31 CE", so the same
> cross-reference yields an assessed present age. **24 at 31 CE gives 36 at
> 43 CE**, which satisfies "30s". Enter it as an assessed rather than confirmed
> figure — that is exactly the confirmed/reported/assessed distinction the
> diagnosis asked for in point 8.

### Correction 2 — "eleven years" contradicts the adjacent paragraph

> **Line, ¶4:** *"Across eleven years of sightings, reports agree on three
> repeated behaviours…"*
> **Against ¶3:** *"Free Latitude dock records first place Senna and the
> fourteenth airframe together in 33 CE."*

33 CE to 43 CE is **ten years**, not eleven. The sample tightened the sourcing
enough to make the arithmetic checkable, which is what exposed it — the original
text carried the same looseness without anchoring it.

> **Required correction.** Either **"ten years of sightings"**, or anchor the
> window to the programme's closure in 32 CE if eleven is wanted. Prefer ten:
> dock records are the stated source and they begin in 33 CE.

### Not a defect — noted so it is not "fixed"

- **`cirrus` ¶2 relocates the Holloway revision** from the pilot entry to the

  permitted. Keep it in both; they may differ on emphasis.
- **"9,000" in digits** against the original's "nine thousand". Approved —
  digits suit an inquiry summary and this is the institutional convention working

### `cirrus` — the one amendment

The register is right and the *execution* is too clean. It reads as a competent
summary **of** rough sources rather than as a rough document. Every sentence is
well-formed, correctly ordered and consistently punctuated. "No two completion
sheets match" and "Several copies repeat that instruction in the margin" are good,
but they are a tidy writer *reporting* mess.

Amendments, all clerical rather than intellectual:

- Let a figure appear twice with different values, uncorrected, and let the note
  say which copy it came from.
- Let one sentence be a fragment or an unpunctuated instruction.
- Let the imperative voice ("Put a target between frame and drone") intrude where
  a specification would not use it — it appears once and should appear more.
- Let a heading or field label survive in the prose, e.g. a bare *weight, final:*
  with no number after it.
- Do **not** introduce misspellings, and do **not** let Holloway look unintelligent.
  She published the revision and was offended it was called impractical.

## 6. Constraint-note review

**Both cited pressures are real.**

The `nulltide` tension — mechanics explicit enough for player verification,
without becoming a hardware assessment or granting interiority — is the hardest
problem in the Archive, and ¶5 solves it by attributing everything to *observed
loadout*. That is the correct general technique and should be reused.

The `stillwake` seam producing deliberate repetition is also real, and the
repetition is **wanted**. Response §5 asked for overlap that is awkward rather
than complementary. Keep it.

**One conflict that should have been raised and was not.** Dropping Senna's
gender and age was a canon-presentation decision made silently. Response §6 listed
exactly this case — *"an entry where the assigned register cannot carry the
required facts"* — and asked for it to be raised rather than solved. The
reconstruction framing made the omission feel natural, which is precisely why it
needed flagging: it is the class of change that looks locally correct and breaks a
global rule. Raise the next one.

## 7. Decision: Route B — approved with local corrections

The calibration succeeds. Apply the four items below, then complete the remaining
seventeen using the amended table in `ARCHIVE_REVIEW_RESPONSE.md` §7.

### Global instructions carried forward

1. **Filing-reference subtitles** as demonstrated. Non-negotiable for all 21.
2. **Separate findings rather than narrated contradiction** — `stillwake` ¶4 is
   the reference implementation for every Concord document.
3. **Attribute observation, never assert interiority.** "Observed loadout",
   "reports agree", "her stated position", "channel recordings show". This
   replaces the old narrator wholesale.
4. **Archive voice only in the four compilation entries** (§2 above). Forbidden
   elsewhere.
5. **Digits and date conventions vary by institution** from a `31 CE` baseline.
6. **Every pilot entry carries gender and age**, in the body where the subtitle is
   a pure filing reference — `pyre` ¶1 is the pattern.
7. **A ledger's last line should be its driest fact**, not its most pointed.
