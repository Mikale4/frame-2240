# Campaign plan — F.R.A.M.E. 2240

Status: **design, approved in outline by the owner (2026-11-21)**. Every name
marked **NEW** below is invented for the campaign and awaits the owner's
approval or a rename — the canon rule is that no new yard, place or named
person enters the setting unreviewed.

## 1. What the owner asked for

- **Eight campaigns, one per pilot**, told in chapters.
- **One connected open world** — not a stage list or a map of levels: every
  chapter of every campaign happens somewhere in the same continuous world,
  and you can walk (and later fly) from any part of it to any other.
- **Voxel, with cutscenes and places to walk around**, the way the FF7 and
  Red Ring Circuit Chao Garden games did.
- **Accounts.** The title screen becomes a login screen and keeps its title
  (the wordmark, PRESS START and the live fight behind them). **Creating an
  account means choosing one pilot**; that pilot is the only one unlocked.
  The other seven unlock by playing.

## 2. Accounts and unlocks

Kept in this browser, as in the owner's other games (Wordle n' Friends,
BLOCKADE, Red Ring Circuit): a callsign, a password of six characters or more
typed twice, salted SHA-256, "remember me", sign out, several accounts per
browser. **A local password is not a security boundary** and the screen says
so in its small print.

Per account: the starter pilot, the unlocked pilots, each pilot's campaign
save (chapter, location, flags), Archive entries read and unlocked, and
lifetime fight records by mode and opponent.

**What locking means.** A locked pilot cannot be chosen for *your* side — in
VERSUS CPU, FREE ORBIT, TRAINING, the player slot of CPU vs CPU, and the
campaign. Rivals are never locked: you have to fight them to earn them. The
TUTORIAL always teaches on HALCYON, unlocked or not.

**Unlock routes** — any one is enough, so nobody is forced down one path:

1. **Story.** Each campaign's chapters bring other pilots in as allies or
   rivals; finishing the chapter that features a pilot unlocks them.
2. **Combat record.** Beat a pilot's machine three times at HARD or above, or
   five times at any skill, in VERSUS CPU or FREE ORBIT.
3. **Rank.** Fights and chapters earn experience; every third rank grants a
   commission — unlock any pilot of your choice.

## 3. The world: the Reach (NEW)

One continuous world at human scale (1 world unit = 1 metre), above the
cloud deck at 43 CE. Yards are platforms at different altitudes, joined by
the **Spans (NEW)** — transit spars and convoy catwalks you walk along, with
lift-trams between altitudes. Fog and the cloud deck hide what is far; each
region streams in as you approach.

| Region | Canon? | What it is | Whose home |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cathedral Yard** | canon | The neutral dispute plate. Night, sodium light, **no people** — the one place disputes are flown. Every campaign's sanctioned duels end up here. | nobody |
| **Brightwake** | canon name, NEW design | Free Latitude docks: berths, salvage yards, a market deck, crews from everywhere. | Holloway, Senna |
| **Meridian (NEW)** | NEW | Concord Skyward's seat: the certification range, the Vantor Aeroworks plant, the instructors' hall where the Archive is kept. | Vesk, Doyne |
| **Coldseam (NEW)** | NEW | Ironwake's raft-cutting platforms at the edge of the storm layer; Pattern 3 walkers on shift. | Okonkwo |
| **Gatewatch (NEW)** | NEW | The three yards Tengku has held for twenty years — Kettle, Bram and Ossory Gates (NEW) — guarding one route. | Tengku |
| **Emberrest (NEW)** | NEW | An Emberline frontier yard; the 7th Assault training ground; the claims office for "the thirteen". | Rook |
| **Cinder Berth (NEW)** | NEW | Ashfall's contract dock, moored under another power's yard and paying rent to all of them. | Volkov |
| **The Spans (NEW)** | NEW | The connective tissue: spars, convoy lanes, tram stations, survey posts. | Doyne's detached duty |

People in the world are **unnamed by role** — a dock steward, a seam
foreman, a range officer — unless the owner names them. The eight pilots are
the only named people.

## 4. The eight campaigns

All eight run through the **same dispute season** in 43 CE: Emberline presses
its claim to the thirteen yards, convoy seizures resume, NULLTIDE is sighted
again, and the standing-rules board starts sending cases to Cathedral Yard.
Campaigns cross: the same event is seen from two sides, and a rival in one is
an ally in another.

Chapter 1 of each is the one being built first: a home region, an opening
cutscene, people to talk to, a sanctioned fight, and the thread that pulls
the pilot out into the Reach.

**Archive tie-in.** Entries start redacted and unlock as campaigns reach
them, in the canon order (EW-14, then Senna, then Rook). Nothing player-facing
explains what 2240 means until the late reveal — the Archive entries that
print the number stay locked until then.

## 5. How it is built

Lessons taken from the owner's FF7 and Red Ring Circuit games (read, not run):

- **Scripts are coroutines** (FF7's `coroutine.js` + `ScriptAPI`): a chapter
  reads as a list of `yield s.say(...)`, `yield s.walk(...)`,
  `yield s.battle(...)`. The main script blocks player control; background
  scripts do not.
- **Places are data**: regions with walkable decks, colliders, NPCs,
  triggers that fire once and remember it, and links later chapters can
  override without editing earlier files.
- **Controller and camera** from the Chao Garden: camera-relative analog
  movement that eases to speed, a step height, an orbit camera that drifts
  behind you and pulls in front of walls, plus FF7's fade for buildings
  between the lens and the pilot.
- **Dialogue**: a typewriter box with two voxel busts (the approved portraits)
  and gestures read from the line.
- **Battles hand off and return**: the walking scene pauses, the side-on
  fight or FREE ORBIT runs on the same renderer, and the script continues
  with the result.
- **Saves**: one state object per pilot per account, filled in from defaults
  on load so old saves survive new fields; autosave on entering a region.
- **Headless validation**: a test dry-runs every chapter script against a
  mock API and checks every link, the FF7 game's `validate.js` in Vitest.

Code layout: `src/account/` (accounts, unlocks — no Phaser), `src/campaign/`
(state, script runner, world data, chapters — no Phaser), `src/world/`
(the Three.js world, figures, camera), `src/scenes/CampaignScene.ts`
(exploration, dialogue and cutscene UI).

## 6. Milestones

1. **Accounts** — login title, account creation with the starter pilot, unlock
   rules and gating in mech select, records from fights. Tests.
2. **Campaign core** — state and saves, the coroutine runner, the world data
   model, walking collision and ground. Tests, including script dry-runs.
3. **The walkable world** — region builders, streaming, the pilot on foot,
   camera, NPCs, prompts, dialogue and cutscenes.
4. **Chapter 1 × 8** — every starter pilot has a playable first chapter.
5. Then: flying your frame between regions, later chapters, the Archive
   unlocks, Stillwake (with the owner).
