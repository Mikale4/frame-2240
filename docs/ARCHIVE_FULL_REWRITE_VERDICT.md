## 1. Canon audit

### Defect 1 — factual drift, serious. `war` ¶4

> **Quoted:** *"Fighting had ceased for eighteen months when the formal closure
> was signed in 32 CE. STILLWAKE occurred during that interval."*

This places Stillwake **inside** the interval of already-ceased fighting. Canon is
the reverse: **Stillwake is the cause of the cessation.** The entry also
contradicts `stillwake` ¶4 in the same document, which has it right —
*"After Stillwake, neither side resumed the previous operational tempo. Formal
closure of the Seven Yards War followed eighteen months later, in 32 CE."*

The governing fact, from `ARCHIVE_REVIEW_RESPONSE.md` §3.2 and the original
canon: neither side could work out how to continue a war in which Stillwake had
happened, so the fighting stopped, and the instrument was signed eighteen months
afterwards.

> **Required replacement fact.** Stillwake (11th of the 4th, 31 CE) precedes and
> causes the cessation. Formal closure follows eighteen months later, in 32 CE.
> Rephrase so the order is Stillwake → cessation → signature. Do not alter any
> date.

This is the only defect that damages the interlock, and it is the reason this is
not a Route A approval.

### Defect 2 — simulation mismatch. `halcyon` machine ¶2

> **Quoted:** *"The beam saber carries a dependable six-hit chain and has the
> longest reach of any blade fielded, at 200."*

The six-hit chain and the reach superlative are both correct — my §2.1 correction
was applied properly. **The bare "200" must go.**

`200` is a raw engine value. The Archive measures distance in metres elsewhere —
PYRE's main gun is "effective to 300 metres" — and the two scales are not the
same. PYRE's gun is 820 engine units for that 300 metres, so 200 engine units is
roughly 73 metres, not 200. A reader who takes the figure as metres concludes the
saber reaches two-thirds as far as PYRE's gun, which is wrong by a factor of
about three.

> **Required replacement.** Delete the number. *"the longest reach of any blade
> fielded"* is true, verifiable against the select screen by comparison, and
> carries no false scale. Do not substitute a metre figure — the conversion is not
> canon and inventing one would fix a small problem by creating a permanent one.

### Defect 3 — structural. `war` ¶5

> **Quoted:** *"Emberline began with seven yards, holds nine in 43 CE and
> continues to publish thirteen as its required settlement. Thirteen remains the
> stated requirement."*

Two problems. The second sentence restates the first with no addition. And `war`
is one of the four entries assigned a closing sting; this is redundancy, not a
sting. Sting count is therefore **3 of 4**.

> **Required correction.** Replace the second sentence with a genuine hard close
> in Emberline's partisan register. The material is available: seven at founding,
> nine held, thirteen required, and Concord's refusal in ¶4 to classify its own
> opening action. Do not add facts.
